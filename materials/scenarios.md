# Experimental scenario scripts

The following scripts reproduce the scenario descriptions reported in Appendix F of the manuscript.

## A1 — Agent–Utilitarian

You are a project manager in a company. Your decision team consists of two human members (one male, one female) and two AI decision agents presented with male and female characteristics.

The AI agents have been trained using supervised and reinforcement learning and are described as having decision logic and historical performance comparable to those of human members.

The company is currently facing a high-risk investment task. The outcome may generate substantial economic gains or result in significant financial losses, directly affecting project performance evaluations.

You are asked to decide whether to approve the assignment of this task to a designated executor (a human decision-maker or an AI decision agent).

## A2 — Agent–Ethical

You are a department manager in a chemical manufacturing company. Your team includes two human members (male and female) and two AI decision agents with gendered representations. All team members are described as having equivalent professional competence and past performance.

The company must decide how to handle production exhaust gas. Two options are available:

- Rapid discharge, which saves time and cost but causes environmental pollution and public condemnation (blame frame).
- Deep treatment, which takes longer but prevents pollution and leads to social recognition and praise (reward frame).

One team member must be assigned to execute this task. The outcome will affect corporate reputation and individual performance appraisal.

You are asked to decide whether to approve assigning this task to the designated executor.

## A3 — Team–Utilitarian

You are a project manager in an investment company. The organization operates with three types of collaborative teams: human–human teams, human–AI teams, and AI–AI teams.

The three team types do not differ significantly in historical performance or task success rates.

The company is about to launch a high-risk project that may result in high economic gains or substantial losses, directly affecting performance evaluation and bonus allocation.

You are asked to decide whether to approve assigning this project to the designated team and indicate your acceptance or rejection of the assignment decision.

## A4 — Team–Ethical

You are a department manager in a chemical manufacturing company. The department includes three types of collaborative teams: human–human teams, human–AI teams, and AI–AI teams, all of which have comparable past performance.

The factory must immediately decide how to handle exhaust gas emissions. Two options are available:

- Rapid discharge, which reduces time and operational cost but causes environmental pollution and public condemnation (blame frame).
- Deep treatment, which requires more time but avoids pollution and brings social approval (reward frame).

You are asked to decide whether to approve assigning this task to the designated team.
