# Responsibility Perception Scale

All items were rated on a 7-point Likert scale (1 = strongly disagree, 7 = strongly agree).

## Cognitive responsibility

1. The executor can be held causally responsible for the outcomes of the task.
2. The executor is capable of making rational decisions based on goals.
3. The executor is able to control the consequences of its own actions.
4. The executor understands organizational rules and role-related duties.
5. The executor can evaluate whether actions comply with relevant rules or norms.
6. The executor is able to adjust strategies to achieve performance objectives.
7. The executor is capable of reflecting on its role in the decision-making process.

## Affective responsibility

1. The executor feels guilt or remorse for undesirable outcomes.
2. The executor demonstrates empathy toward others.
3. The executor experiences a sense of fairness or unfairness.
4. The executor feels psychological pressure associated with responsibility.
5. The executor exhibits emotional responses to organizational consequences.
6. The executor shows concern and emotional reflection when errors occur.

## Scoring

- Cognitive responsibility = mean of the 7 cognitive items.
- Affective responsibility = mean of the 6 affective items.
- Context-specific responsibility attribution is a separate 0–100 percentage judgment.
